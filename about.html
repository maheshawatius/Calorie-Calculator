THE PERSON BEHIND THE COACHING

Real life comes first.

Changing your health habits can be hard to sustain when a plan asks you to change everything at once. My approach is to make the next step feel clear and manageable, then build from there.

We’ll focus on what matters to you, make space for your routines and preferences, and use progress as information—not a reason to judge yourself. The goal is a healthier way of living you can keep practicing over time.
